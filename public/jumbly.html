How to Play Jumbly

Six letters are jumbled up. Find every word hidden inside them.

  • Tap letters (or type) to spell a word, then press Enter.
  • Words are 3 to 6 letters long. Each letter can be used once per word.
  • The empty boxes show how many words are left and how long each one is.
  • At least one word uses all six letters — that's the big one.
  • Real words that aren't on the board still count as bonus words (half points).
  • Scoring: 10 points per letter, and the six-letter word is worth 100.
  • Mix up shuffles the letters if you're stuck. Hint shows the first letter of a word you haven't found.

Daily gives everyone the same letters, resetting at midnight. Practice gives a new puzzle whenever you like. There's no timer — take your time.

Keyboard: type letters, Enter to submit, Backspace to delete, Space to mix.
