    How to Play Word Pond

    Take turns with Quackers making words on the board, crossword-style. Highest score wins.

    • Tap one of your tiles, then tap a square to put it there. Tap a tile you just placed to take it back.
    • On a computer you can also click a square and type.
    • All your tiles in a turn go in one straight line, and must join up with tiles already on the board. The first word covers the center star.
    • Every word your tiles make must be a real word — including words formed sideways.
    • Each tile shows its points. Colored squares multiply them: DL/TL double or triple a letter, DW/TW double or triple the whole word.
    • Use all 7 tiles in one turn for a 40-point bonus.
    • The two blank tiles can be any letter (worth 0).
    • Hint shows a good move (3 per game). Swap trades tiles for new ones; Pass skips your turn.
    • The game ends when the tiles run out and someone empties their rack, or after both players pass twice in a row. Leftover tiles count against you.

    Your game saves automatically — come back any time.
